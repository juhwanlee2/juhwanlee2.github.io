About me

I'm a Ph.D. student and research assistant in Air Transportation at Korea Aerospace University. My research interests include airport operations, air traffic management, aviation simulation, and decision support systems.

Airports change minute by minute as aircraft, passengers, and resources interact. I'm interested in understanding how these interactions shape operational performance and how data can help airports anticipate problems before they develop into disruptions. My research draws on airport operations analysis, passenger queue forecasting, and aircraft trajectory analysis to understand how this changing system behaves.

Through my doctoral research, I aim to develop predictive models that use operational data to anticipate congestion and emerging risks in airport operations while explicitly accounting for uncertainty in those predictions. By providing both forecasts and an understanding of their reliability, I hope to support proactive airport operations that remain safe and efficient as conditions change, improving the experience of the people who operate and use them.

Research interests: airport operations, air traffic management, aviation simulation, decision support systems, and uncertainty-aware forecasting.

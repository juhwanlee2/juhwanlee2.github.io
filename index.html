Understanding airports.
Informing better decisions.

I am a Ph.D. student and research assistant in Air Transportation at Korea Aerospace University. My research focuses on airport operations, passenger terminal simulation, and data-driven queue forecasting.

I combine simulation and machine learning to study how airports respond to changing demand and operational constraints. My work includes capacity and delay analysis, passenger congestion modeling, and Transformer-based forecasting, with the goal of supporting better operational decisions.

I received my M.S. and B.S. in Air Transportation from Korea Aerospace University in 2023 and 2021, respectively.

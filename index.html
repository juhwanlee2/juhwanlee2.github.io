Juhwan Lee

Air Transportation · Korea Aerospace University

I am a Ph.D. student and research assistant in Air Transportation at Korea Aerospace University. I study airport operations, passenger terminal simulation, and data-driven modeling for aviation systems and air traffic management (ATM).

My research combines simulation and machine learning to understand how airports respond to changing demand and operational constraints. I work on capacity and delay analysis, passenger congestion modeling, and Transformer-based forecasting to support airport operations and resource allocation.

I received my Master's and Bachelor's degrees in Air Transportation from Korea Aerospace University, where I completed my Master's under the supervision of Hojong Baik.

Research Keywords: airport operations, capacity analysis, passenger terminal simulation, queue forecasting, AI-enabled decision support
